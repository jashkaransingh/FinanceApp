# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2025-08-25_

This Policy explains how **Finance App** (“we”, “us”) handles your data.

## Data We Collect
- **Account Data**: email, name (if provided), sign-in metadata.
- **Device/Usage Data**: app version, crash/diagnostic info.
- **Linked Accounts**: via **Plaid**. We never see your bank credentials; Plaid exchanges tokens.

## How We Use Data
- Provide core features (auth, bank linking, budgets).
- Improve reliability, debug issues, and secure the service.
- Communicate important changes (never spam).

## Sharing
- **Processors** we use to run the app:
  - **Firebase** (Authentication, possibly Analytics/Crash reporting)
  - **Google Sign-In**
  - **Plaid** (bank linking)
- We do **not** sell personal data.

## Retention & Security
- We keep data as long as needed for the App, then delete or anonymize.
- We take reasonable measures to protect data; no method is 100% secure.

## Your Choices
- You can disconnect bank accounts, delete your account, or request data deletion at **privacy@example.com**.

## Children
- Not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
- We’ll update this page and change the “last updated” date.

## Contact
- **privacy@example.com**
